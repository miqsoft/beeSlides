## Start

<Tabs syncKey="app">
  <TabItem label="Desktop app">
    Click **Present** in the title bar, or press <kbd>F5</kbd>. With a second
    screen the slides go there and the speaker view opens on yours.
    **Play ▸ Present in tab** shows the slides inside the window instead.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Browser">
    Click **Present** (or **Play ▸ Present**) for fullscreen, or
    **Play ▸ Present in tab** to fill just the browser tab.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Speaker view

Your notes, a timer and the next slide. Press <kbd>S</kbd> while presenting to
open it, then drag it to the screen you look at. Write the notes in the
[Speaker notes pane](../../create/edit/#speaker-notes).

## Six keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>→</kbd> / <kbd>←</kbd> | Next / previous |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Speaker view |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | Laser pointer |
| <kbd>P</kbd> | Pen (draw on the slide) |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | End the show |

The rest are in [Keyboard shortcuts](../../reference/shortcuts/#presenting).

**Kiosk:** **Play ▸ Kiosk (self-running)** moves through the slides on a timer, for a
screen in a lobby or at a booth.
