Comments live **inside the deck file**. Send someone the deck, they comment
and send it back, and the threads are there. They never show while
presenting or in a PDF.

## Add, reply, resolve

- **Add:** click **Comment** (Home tab), press <kbd>C</kbd>, or choose
  **Insert ▸ Comment**, then click an element or a spot on the slide. Type in
  *Add a comment…* and click **Comment**.
- **Reply:** open the thread and type in *Reply…*.
- **Resolve:** the **Resolve** button closes a thread; **Reopen** brings it
  back.
- **Your name:** the thread's **More options** menu has **Set your name…**,
  shown on new comments.

## Hand them to Claude

<Tabs syncKey="app">
  <TabItem label="Desktop app">
    **Send to Claude** (Home tab, or **Play ▸ Send to Claude**) hands every
    open comment to Claude Code, which works through them in the deck. A
    thread it finishes says *Claude marked this done — please check*:
    **Accept** it or **Reopen** it. A single thread also has its own
    **Send to Claude** button.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Browser">
    The browser editor has no Send to Claude. Comments are part of the deck's
    JSON, so the [chatbot route](../../create/ai/#in-the-browser-any-chatbot)
    carries them along.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
