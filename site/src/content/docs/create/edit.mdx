<kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS is <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> on Windows and Linux. The menu paths
below are the desktop app's menu bar; the ribbon has the same commands.

## Slides

- **Add:** **＋ New slide** below the slide list, then pick a slide type. With
  the list focused, <kbd>Enter</kbd> adds one too.
- **Duplicate:** <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> in the slide list.
- **Reorder:** drag a slide in the list, or use the
  [Slide Sorter](../editor/#three-views) for many at once.
- **Hide:** **Slide ▸ Hide slide** keeps a slide in the deck but skips it when
  you present.

## Insert

The **Insert** tab (or menu) adds **Text**, **Image**, **Shape**, **Icons**,
**Table**, **Chart**, **Media** (video or audio), **Equation** and **Code**.
Each lands on the current slide, ready to move and format.

## Select and format

- Click to select, <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click to add to the selection, <kbd>Esc</kbd>
  to deselect. Double-click text to type in it.
- The **slide bar** under the slide shows quick formatting for what's
  selected.
- The **properties panel** on the right has everything else about it. With
  nothing selected it shows the slide: background, transition, theme colours.
- **Format Painter** (Home tab) copies one element's look onto others.

## Arrange

- **Arrange ▸ Align** lines up the selection (left, centre, top…), and
  **Arrange ▸ Distribute** spaces it evenly.
- **Group** with <kbd>G</kbd>, ungroup with <kbd>⇧</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd>.
- Arrow keys nudge by 1 px, <kbd>⇧</kbd>+arrows by 10 px.

## Undo

<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes, <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>⇧</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>
redoes.

## Speaker notes

Type them in the **Speaker notes** pane under the slide. Only you see them,
in the speaker view while [presenting](../../present/present/#speaker-view).
