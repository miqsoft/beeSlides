Two words, two levels, the same idea as PowerPoint's slide layouts and
templates:

| BeeSlides | PowerPoint | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Slide type** | slide layout / master | a slide design *inside* a deck — "Title", "Two columns", "Section" — with placeholders and fixed decoration |
| **Layout** | template (`.potx`) | a reusable *starter file*: slide types, theme, fonts. Every deck made from it is new and independent |

## Slide types in a deck

- **New slide** offers the deck's slide types to start from.
- **Slide ▸ Change slide type…** switches a slide to another type; its
  content moves into the matching placeholders.
- **Slide ▸ Layout Manager…** edits the slide types themselves. A change there
  reaches every slide of that type at once: the logo, the footer, the title
  position.

Slides stay linked to their type; anything you place on a slide yourself stays
yours.

## Layouts: start new decks from a design

<Steps>
1. **Make one.** In a deck whose design you like, choose **Save as layout…**
   (in the **▾** next to Save). It saves the slide types, theme and fonts,
   not your content.
2. **Use it.**
   <Tabs syncKey="app">
     <TabItem label="Desktop app">
       Your layouts are on the start page under **Layouts**: one click starts a
       new deck from one. To make one the default, open its **⋯** menu and choose
       **Use as default**; **File ▸ New from Default Layout** then starts from it.
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Browser">
       A layout is a file. Opening it gives you a fresh, independent deck.
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Your company template">
  In the desktop app's start page, **Layouts ▸ From a file…** turns a
  PowerPoint template (`.pptx` or `.potx`) into a BeeSlides layout, and
  **PowerPoint with AI…** has Claude rebuild its design. **＋ Create layout…**
  starts one from scratch.
</Aside>

## Import PowerPoint

<Tabs syncKey="app">
  <TabItem label="Desktop app">
    On the start page, under *New deck*, click **From a file…** or drop the
    `.pptx` anywhere on the page. In an open deck: **File ▸ Import ▸ Import
    PowerPoint…**. **PowerPoint with AI…** has Claude Code rebuild it as a
    native, editable deck instead of converting it shape by shape.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Browser">
    **File ▸ Import ▸ Import PowerPoint…** and pick the `.pptx`. It is converted
    right in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The slides come in with their text, pictures, shapes and tables. Masters and
layouts become **slide types**, still linked, so the Layout Manager changes
every slide that uses one. Theme colours and fonts come along. Text that would
overflow is fixed in one step you can undo; anything that can't be fixed
faithfully is listed for you (desktop: hand the list to Claude).
