![The BeeSlides desktop app with a deck open. Numbered markers: 1 title bar, 2 ribbon tabs, 3 ribbon, 4 slide list, 5 canvas, 6 slide bar, 7 properties panel, 8 speaker notes.](/beeslides/shots/editor.png)

1. **Title bar**: from left to right, Home (the start page), **Save** with its
   **▾** menu, undo and redo, then the deck's name and save state. On the
   right are **Present**, the **Claude Code terminal**, **Settings** and the
   button that folds the ribbon away.
2. **Ribbon tabs**: **Home**, **Insert**, **Design**, **Transitions** and
   **View**. In the desktop app the same commands are also in the menu bar
   (File, Edit, Insert, Slide, Arrange, View, Play).
3. **Ribbon**: the tools of the chosen tab. **Insert** adds text, shapes,
   pictures, media, tables, charts, equations, code and icons.
4. **Slide list**: click a slide to open it, drag to reorder, **＋ New slide**
   at the bottom. With the list focused, <kbd>Enter</kbd> adds a slide and
   <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> duplicates one.
5. **Canvas**: the slide you're editing. Click to select, double-click text to
   type; <kbd>Esc</kbd> deselects. The zoom control sits at its bottom right.
6. **Slide bar**: the current slide's background, transition and slide type,
   and another **New slide**. When something is selected it shows that
   element's quick formatting instead.
7. **Properties panel**: everything about what's selected. With nothing
   selected it shows the **slide**: page size, background, transition,
   slideshow options and the deck's theme colours.
8. **Speaker notes**: notes for this slide. Only you see them, in the speaker
   view while presenting.

## Three views

**View** (menu bar or ribbon tab) switches how you see the deck:

- **Normal**: the window above, for working on one slide at a time.
- **Slide Sorter**: every slide as a thumbnail. Drag to reorder;
  <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>- or <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click to pick several, then
  **Duplicate** or **Delete**. Click a slide to open it.

  ![Slide Sorter: the deck's slides as a grid of thumbnails.](/beeslides/shots/view-sorter.png)
- **Outline View**: the deck as text, one slide title and its text per row.
  Good for writing the story before the design.

  ![Outline View: slide titles and their text as an editable list.](/beeslides/shots/view-outline.png)

<kbd>Esc</kbd> or **✕** goes back to Normal.

## Show and hide panels

**View ▸ Slides**, **View ▸ Format** and **View ▸ Notes** show or hide the
slide list, the properties panel and the speaker notes. The same three buttons
are in the **View** ribbon tab under **Panels**. **View ▸ Ribbon** folds the
ribbon away.

<Aside type="tip" title="Language">
  The interface comes in English, 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, Français,
  Italiano, Deutsch and Português: **View ▸ Language** in the desktop app, the
  globe button in the browser. It never changes what's in your deck.
</Aside>
