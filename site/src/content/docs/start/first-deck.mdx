A quick tour that touches everything once. Each step links to the page that
covers it properly.

<Steps>
1. **Start a deck.**
   <Tabs syncKey="app">
     <TabItem label="Desktop app">
       The app opens on the start page. Under **New deck**, click **Blank**
       (one empty slide) or one of your layouts.
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Browser">
       Open the [blank editor](/beeSlides/app/BeeSlides.beeslides.html). It
       starts with one empty slide.
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>
2. **Add a slide.** Click **＋ New slide** below the slide list and pick
   **Title**. [Edit by hand](../../create/edit/) covers text, pictures,
   shapes and more.
3. **Type a title.** Click *Click to add title* and type.
   [The editor](../../create/editor/) explains the rest of the window.
4. **Present.** Click **Present** in the title bar (desktop: <kbd>F5</kbd>).
   <kbd>→</kbd> moves on, <kbd>Esc</kbd> ends the show. See
   [Present](../../present/present/).
5. **Save.** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> and choose where
   to put the file. See [Save and share](../../share/save/).
6. **Send it.** The `.beeslides.html` file *is* the deck. Whoever gets it
   opens it in a browser, with nothing to install.
</Steps>
